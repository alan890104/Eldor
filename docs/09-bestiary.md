# 09 New Spirit Roster (Second Batch: 38 Spirits)

> The first batch of 26 spirits is listed in the Spirit Codex (`npm run report` writes it to `docs/catalog/spirits.md`). With this batch there are **64 spirits** in total.
> **Every kit has been redone**: each spirit now has its own signature mechanic and a learnset gained by level (see the [10 Kit Authoring Guide](10-kit-authoring.md)). The background stories in this document are still valid, but the "flagship skills" and some of the traits below are from the earlier design. The current kits, mechanics and traits are those in the Spirit Codex, generated by `npm run report`.
> Every spirit has its own story, one unique trait and one flagship skill; the flagship skills use the battle mechanics added in the second batch (§2).

## 1. Design Principles

1. **One spirit, one story**: each background is tied to a region's culture or to a historical event, so reading it tells you where the spirit sits in the world.
2. **One spirit, one playstyle**: no trait is repeated, and the flagship skill gives the spirit a clear job on the team.
3. **Every region has its own ecology**: each of the eight element regions gains 4 spirits (N ×2, R ×1, SR ×1), so exploration turns up different spirits; Astralgate gains 2; and there are 4 more legendary SSRs.
4. **Every new mechanic has an answer**: each new mechanic is listed with its counters (§2).

## 2. Battle Mechanics Added in the Second Batch

| Mechanic | Description | Counters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Armored Offense (以守為攻) | Damage is calculated from the user's own Defense instead of its Attack | Opponents with high Resistance are unaffected; dispel Defense boosts |
| Borrowed Force (借力) | Damage is calculated from the opponent's Attack | Lower your own Attack before entering the field |
| Refraction (折射) | Damage is calculated against the lower of the opponent's Defense and Resistance | Spirits with balanced defenses |
| Streak (連環) | Using the same skill in consecutive turns multiplies its power by ×1.5 each time (up to 3 times) | Guard (格擋) to break the rhythm; switching |
| Fate (命運) | Power becomes a random ×0.5 to ×2 | None |
| Self-Destruct (自爆) | The user faints after attacking | Guard (the user still faints) |
| Echo (回音) | Hits again at the end of the next turn (50% power) | Switching, shields |
| Entangle (纏繞) | For 3 turns the target cannot switch, and loses a set percentage of its HP at the end of each turn | Cleanse (淨化), or knock out the caster |
| Aether Drain (汲取靈能) | The opponent loses Aether (靈能) and the caster gains the same amount | Spend your Aether first |
| Exposed (破綻) | The next attack received within 2 turns is a guaranteed crit | Guard, switching, Hard Shell (硬殼) |
| Channel (傾注) | Spends all remaining Aether, and power rises for each point spent | Keep the opponent short on Aether |
| Burrow (潛地) | The charging turn is spent underground, where attacks cannot hit; the user strikes on the next turn | Guard, set a Trap (伏技), switch |
| Wish (許願) | At the end of the next turn, our active spirit recovers HP | Curse (詛咒), or knock it out before the wish lands |
| Prophecy (預言) | After 2 turns, the opponent's active spirit takes damage equal to a set percentage of its max HP | Switch to a spirit with plenty of HP |
| Advance (預支) | Gain Aether immediately; afterwards the Aether recovered at the end of each turn is reduced (-1 per advance, stacking, down to a minimum of 0) | Pressure the opponent while they repay: after their Ultimate (奧義), they will struggle to rebuild Aether for several turns |
| Decoy (替身) | Pays HP to create a decoy; the decoy takes attacks and blocks the status conditions and debuffs of the opponent's Status (秘術) skills | Multi-hit attacks, Prophecy and damage over time |
| Seal (封印) | The opponent's last-used skill cannot be used for 2 turns | Carry a variety of skills |
| Copy / Steal / Invert (臨摹／竊取／反轉) | Copy the opponent's boosts, take the opponent's boosts, or flip the signs of the opponent's stat stages | Do not boost; attack first, boost afterwards |
| Baton Pass (接力) | Switches out and hands its stat stages and shield to the spirit coming in | Dispel, Invert, forced switch |
| Linked Fate (同命) | If the user is knocked out before its next action, the spirit that knocked it out faints too | Wait a turn before attacking; finish with damage over time |
| HP Split (均分) | Both sides' HP is averaged proportionally | It only backfires when the opponent's HP is high |
| Perish (終焉) | After 3 turns both active spirits faint | Switching out removes it |
| Scapegoat (嫁禍) | Transfers the user's own status conditions to the opponent | Element immunity, Sanctuary (聖域) |
| Counter Stance (反擊架勢) | If attacked this turn, the damage is reflected | Use Status skills or set a trap instead |
| Entry Hazard (入場陷阱) | Lays a trap on the opponent's side that triggers every time they switch in | A team-wide Cleanse removes it; switch less |
| Crystal Wall / Light Veil (晶壁／光幕) | Reduces Physical (武技) / Magic (魔法) damage taken on our side for 5 turns | Dispel has no effect, so you can only outlast the turns; attack with the other type |
| Life Song (生命之歌) | The user faints, and the next ally to enter is fully healed and cleansed | Prophecy, Entry Hazard |

### Astralgate (星門城)

#### Mainspring King (機樞鼠王)  `clockmouse`
- Mainspring King | Neutral | N | Rush, Streak
- **Background**: In the Kingdom Age (王國紀), craftsmen built mice that wind themselves in order to maintain the Star Gate (星門). After the Night of the Shattered Heart (碎心之夜) the craftsmen vanished, but the mice still live among the Star Gate's gears, winding their springs generation after generation.
- **Trait [Wind-Up (上發條)]**: At the end of a turn in which it did not switch, Speed +1 stage (at most +2 stages from this trait).
- **Flagship "Windup Dash (發條衝刺)"**: Neutral, Physical. "Streak": power ×1.5 each time it is used in consecutive turns.
- **Model**: A small four-legged mouse in bronze `#b08a5a` with a gray belly, big round ears, and a spinning windup key stuck in its back. Stage II: two turning gears on its shoulders, and a spring at the tip of its tail. Stage III: an upright Mouse King with a gear crown and a short red cape, holding a windup-key scepter.

#### Scroll Cat (繪卷靈貓)  `inkcat`
- Scroll Cat | Neutral | R | Disruption, Invert
- **Background**: It lives in the scriptorium of the Star Vault Sages' Tower (星穹賢者塔), and its inky footprints turn into little moving paintings. The sages found that what it draws is often what someone else is thinking.
- **Trait [Copy]**: On entering, copies the opponent's current positive stat stages.
- **Flagship "Ink Rewrite (塗改)"**: Neutral, Status. Inverts the signs of the opponent's stat stages, and raises its own Magic Power by 1 stage.
- **Model**: A white cat `#f4f0e6` with black ink spots `#1c1a24`, its tail tip like an ink-dipped brush (a black cone with dripping ink beads). Stage II: the spots join into flowing stripes, and an unrolled scroll floats behind it. Stage III: more slender, with two scrolls spread like wings, and the brush tail gives off a faint blue glow `#6ab8ff`.

### Aether Supply (Addendum)

Originally the only spirit that could gain Aether repeatedly was Cloudfluff (絨雲), with its "Meditate (冥想)". The two spirits below give a team a dedicated charger: send one in first to build up Aether, then switch to the main attacker to cast the Ultimate (Aether is shared by the whole team).

#### Starwell Lotus Maiden (星泉蓮姬)  `springlotus`
- Starwell Lotus Maiden | Water / Nature | R | Support, Aether Supply
- **Background**: Deep in Whisperwood lies an ancient spring; since the Night of the Shattered Heart, what flows in it is no longer water but scattered Aether. The spring-heart lotus took root over the spring's eye and hands the Aether out bit by bit to passing Pactbearers. After battles the spirits queue up to drink at the spring, and no one has ever cut in line.
- **Trait [Wellspring (湧泉)]**: While it is on the field, our side gains 1 extra Aether at the end of each turn.
- **Exclusive "Aether Infusion (靈泉灌注)"** (Lv 5): Water, Status, cost 0. Our Aether +3, and its own Speed is -1 stage.
- **Model**: A light blue and tender green lotus spirit, with a glowing spring droplet `#8fe8ff` cradled in the heart of the flower; in Stage III the petals open into a skirt, and a crown of water droplets floats above its head.

#### Thunderplate (雷鎧犰狳)  `voltadillo`
- Thunderplate | Thunder / Earth | N | Tank, Energy Storage
- **Background**: On stormy nights, the armadillos of the plains curl into balls and let the lightning strike their shells. The more they are struck, the brighter the lines on their shells glow, and the herders call this light "stored thunder". A Pactbearer brings it into battle not to win, but to take hits for teammates and store Aether for them.
- **Trait [Storage Plating (蓄能背甲)]**: After being attacked, our side gains 1 Aether (at most once per turn).
- **Exclusive "Curl and Charge (蜷身蓄電)"** (Lv 5): Thunder, Status, cost 1. Defense +1 stage, and our Aether +2.
- **Model**: A gray-blue armadillo `#6f7f9a` with a segmented shell and yellow electric light `#ffe05a` glowing in the seams; when curled up, it is a ball of electricity.
